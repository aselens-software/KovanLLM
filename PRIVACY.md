# Privacy Policy for KovanLLM

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

**KovanLLM** ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by **Aselens Software℠** (https://aselens.com), is designed with privacy and local computing at its core. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the KovanLLM mobile application and desktop station.

---

## 1. Local Processing Architecture (Zero Cloud Storage of Chats)

- **Local LLM Conversations:** KovanLLM connects your mobile device directly to your personal local desktop station (LM Studio / local LLM engine). All prompts, conversation histories, attached media, and generated tokens are processed on your local machine and stored locally on your mobile device. We do not operate intermediate servers that collect, store, or inspect your private conversations.
- **Direct Encrypted Tunnels:** When connecting remotely via Cloudflare Quick Tunnel (`*.trycloudflare.com`), communication is encrypted in transit over HTTPS between your mobile device and your station daemon.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Used by the Application

- **Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Used exclusively for real-time optical scanning of pairing QR codes generated by your desktop station. The camera stream is analyzed on-device using Google Code Scanner. No images or camera feeds are captured, saved, or uploaded to any server.
- **Audio Permission (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used strictly when you activate the live voice assistant mode. Audio is transcribed on-device using Android's native SpeechRecognizer. Voice audio is not sent to external telemetry servers.
- **Photo/Media Access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`):** Used solely when you choose to attach images, documents, or media to a chat prompt. Selected files are converted on-device to base64 and transmitted directly to your paired local station.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The KovanLLM Android application displays banner and inline promotional advertisements through **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**.

### Information Collected Automatically by AdMob:
Google AdMob may collect and process certain information in accordance with its own privacy policies:
- **Device Identifiers:** Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, device model, operating system version, and mobile network information.
- **Ad Interaction Data:** Views, clicks, impressions, and diagnostic/performance metrics.

### Consent & Regulatory Compliance (GDPR / KVKK / CCPA):
In jurisdictions requiring consent (such as the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Turkey under KVKK), KovanLLM utilizes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain required user consent for personalized or non-personalized advertising before ad requests are served.
You can review Google's privacy practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical safeguards:
- **Station Authentication:** Station endpoints are protected with session tokens and PIN verification to block unauthorized remote connections.
- **Encrypted In-Transit:** Communications over public networks utilize TLS/HTTPS.
- **Data Minimization:** No personal account registration, email addresses, or phone numbers are collected by KovanLLM.
